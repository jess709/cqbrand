Portfolio

Five companies. One operating philosophy.

I've built five companies across private capital, real estate, lending, accounting, and marketing. They may look different on paper, but there is a common thread: I saw a problem, learned the economics behind it, and built a business to solve it.

Real estate was the starting point, but the portfolio has grown well beyond owning property. Today, I'm involved in businesses that deploy capital, finance real estate investors, operate housing, provide financial clarity to business owners, and help companies build their marketing function.
