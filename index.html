My Story

From CPA to entrepreneur to investor.

In 2019, I got let go from my job as CFO. I had a decision to make: find another corporate job or build something of my own. I chose the second option.

A few weeks later, my business partner and I bought our first property: a $40,000 single-family home in Allentown, Pittsburgh. I never set out to become a real estate professional. That first property became the starting point for what I've built since.

Today, I'm still an operator first. I've built companies, invested in real estate, managed people, and deployed capital. That experience shapes how I evaluate opportunities today.

SteelPoint Capital Holdings

SteelPoint Capital Holdings is the platform I've built to invest in and operate businesses and real estate. I work with private investors who want to understand where their capital is going, how opportunities are evaluated, and who is responsible for the decisions behind it.

I'm actively building relationships with investors who take a long-term view and want to learn more about what I'm doing at SteelPoint.
